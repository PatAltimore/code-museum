    content: "A SYNTAX form has the verb, then up to two OBJECT slots, each optionally preceded by a preposition. After OBJECT come two parenthesised lists. The first, introduced by FIND, names an attribute the parser may use to guess a missing object, which is the GWIM bit: FIND LIGHTBIT means 'if the player didn't say what to light, look for something lightable'. The second list gives where to look and what to require: HELD, CARRIED, ON-GROUND and IN-ROOM define the search scope, HAVE demands that the player already hold the object, TAKE asks the game to try to pick it up first, and MANY allows several objects. The equals sign is followed by the routine, and optionally a pre-action routine that runs earlier. ATTACK appears twice: in Zork II a conditional adds a form without a weapon, so a bare 'attack' of a creature is accepted without asking 'with what?', while the with-weapon form requires WEAPONBIT."
